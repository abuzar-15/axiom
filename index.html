Keyboard Shortcuts & Accessibility Guide

Screen Reader & Accessibility Guide

Axiom is engineered with universal accessibility standards. Every button, tool, and indicator provides an unambiguous accessible name. Results and formula updates are voiced automatically via screen reader live regions. You can press D at any time to hear the current display value, or F for the full formula expression.

Desktop Screen Readers: Turn Off Browse Mode for Direct Keyboard Typing

To type numbers, mathematical operations, and scientific functions directly with your computer keyboard without interference from screen reader single-letter navigation keys:

  • NVDA (Windows): Press NVDA + Space (or Insert + Space) to toggle into Focus Mode. You will hear an activation click. In Focus Mode, all keys (0–9, +, -, *, /, Enter, =) pass directly to the calculator. Press NVDA + Space again to return to Browse Mode.
  • JAWS (Windows): Press Insert + Z to toggle the Virtual PC Cursor off. In Forms/PC Cursor mode, all keyboard keys type directly into Axiom. Press Insert + Z again to re-enable the Virtual PC Cursor.
  • Default Keyboard Focus: Whenever Axiom loads, focus automatically lands on the calculator display. You can immediately begin typing calculations right away.

Audio Tone Legend & Sound Samples

Axiom generates distinct procedural audio tones to provide immediate feedback for every action. Activate any button below to listen to that sound sample:
